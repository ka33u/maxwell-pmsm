# Ideal branch winding diagnostic

For a complete, explicitly supplied two-layer winding table, `scripts/ideal_winding_phasors.py` provides an optional **ideal fundamental** screening of individual parallel branches before building a Maxwell case. It does not read the Maxwell project or establish the actual coil terminals, branch routing, magnetic field, connection, branch impedance or circulating currents.

The CSV header must be exactly `coil,phase,parallel_branch,upper_slot,upper_polarity,lower_slot,lower_polarity,series_turns_per_coil`. It requires one coil per slot, one upper and lower side per slot, coil IDs and slots numbered 1 through Q, phases A/B/C, branch numbers 1 through the specified number, positive integer turns and opposite `+`/`-` side polarities. Every phase/branch must be nonempty. Each lower slot must equal upper slot plus the explicit positive pitch modulo Q. It is not a parser for arbitrary winding diagrams, single-layer windings or alternative numbering conventions; adapt and validate such inputs separately rather than forcing this format. [Ansys's 2026 R1 2D transient winding documentation](https://ansyshelp.ansys.com/public/views/secured/electronics/v261/en/subsystems/maxwell/content/maxwell/AssigningaWindingSetupfora2DTransientSolver.htm) distinguishes a winding from its assigned coil terminals and conductors; confirm the installed solver version's own conventions.

```powershell
& $motorPython '<skill>\scripts\ideal_winding_phasors.py' --csv '<candidate.csv>' --slots <Q> --poles <even-poles> --pitch <slots> --branches <per-phase> --out '<new-output.json>'
```

With uniform slot pitch and a common sinusoidal fundamental, define `theta(k)=2*pi*(poles/2)*(k-1)/Q` and `K_branch=sum N*(s_upper*exp(j*theta_upper)+s_lower*exp(j*theta_lower))`. Here `N` is each coil's series turns, and `s` is its CSV side sign. If `psi(t)=Re(K*exp(j*omega*t))` then the ideal induced-EMF coefficient is `j*omega*K` under that convention. Slot 1 and time zero are arbitrary common references. K is a relative phasor, **not volts**. Side polarity must be encoded consistently with the intended direction of each branch's series connection. The table alone does not prove that convention matches the actual motor.

The JSON includes each branch's complex K, magnitude, angle, counts and turns, per-phase mean and deviations, and relative phase-mean angles. When the mean is effectively zero relative to branch magnitudes, the normalized deviation and mean angle are `null`; inspect absolute deviations. No acceptance threshold is imposed. The sign of an interphase spatial angle in this convention is not by itself a verified real time-domain phase sequence. A branch polarity reversal can be detected under the assumed convention, but no deviation does not prove a correct winding or absence of branch currents. Confirm branch polarity, physical turn counts, factory connections, and actual field/circuit effects separately.
